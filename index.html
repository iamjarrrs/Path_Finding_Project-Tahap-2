Key AI Search Insights (From Lecture Slides)

• UCS (Uniform Cost Search) expands in concentric circles (order of g) without knowing where the goal is. In this larger 56×40 map, UCS often expands 1,000+ nodes, flooding the terrain.
• A* Search with Manhattan achieves optimality and significantly prunes the search space because Manhattan distance is an admissible and consistent heuristic for 4-directional movement.
• Heuristic Dominance: Because Manhattan distance h_m(n) ≥ h_e(n) (Euclidean), Manhattan dominates Euclidean and expands significantly fewer nodes!
• Greedy Best-First only considers h(n), which makes it fast in open fields but causes it to take suboptimal detours or become trapped when facing concave obstacles (like the U-trap).
